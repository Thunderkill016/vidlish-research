## Initial concepts imported

- CEFR reception / production / interaction / mediation framing.
- `understood` / `recalled` / `transferred` / `retained` evidence separation.
- A0 audio/text first; authentic video later behind a difficulty gate.
- vocabulary/chunks in meaningful contexts rather than alphabetical/POS lists.
- retrieval and delayed review without equating FSRS/schedule state with mastery.
- listening as perception + segmentation + comprehension, not passive exposure.
- controlled speaking before broad free conversation near A0.
- intelligibility-first pronunciation and caution with ASR grading.
- captions as scaffold, not proof that watching a video teaches the target.
- AI as a bounded generator/feedback tool with provenance and evaluation gates.

## Migration rule

When an imported statement receives a dedicated verified research note here, update the product codebase to reference its stable ID and avoid duplicating a long rationale in both repositories.
