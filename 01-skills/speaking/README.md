# Speaking research

Nếp treats early speaking as a **graduated production problem**, not a binary choice between listen-and-repeat and free conversation.

Research basis for the initial near-A0 policy:

- `SYN-SPK-001`
- `FEAT-SPK-001`
- `EXP-006`
- `CLM-SPK-001` through `CLM-SPK-012`

## Working speaking ladder

```text
SP0 rehearsal
  listen-and-repeat / shadow / read aloud
        ↓
SP1 bounded oral completion
        ↓
SP2 full short-utterance retrieval
        ↓
SP3 controlled recombination
        ↓
SP4 guided one-turn interaction
        ↓
SP5 bounded multi-turn interaction / role-play
        ↓
SP6 open interaction, later and sampled
```

The ladder is product metadata, not a claim that speaking acquisition always proceeds through universal psychological stages or that an `SP` level maps directly onto CEFR.

## Evidence boundary

```text
model/script visible while speaking
→ rehearsal / supported production

model hidden before response
+ attempt before feedback
+ support provenance recorded
→ candidate independent speaking evidence
```

A corrected repetition after feedback is useful practice but does not overwrite the learner's original failed independent attempt.

## What to measure

Speaking evidence should remain task-specific, for example:

- `oral_target_recall`
- `short_utterance_production`
- `controlled_recombination`
- `guided_turn_response`
- `interaction_task_success`
- `interaction_repair`
- `speaking_transfer`

Fluency, pronunciation, grammatical accuracy and interaction are related but should not be collapsed into one unvalidated score.

## Open dependencies

- `RQ-007`: pronunciation targets that materially affect intelligibility for Vietnamese L1 learners.
- `RQ-008`: which ASR/speech measures are reliable enough for this population and task set.

Until those cycles are complete, Nếp must not use an opaque ASR/pronunciation score as a speaking-mastery gate.
