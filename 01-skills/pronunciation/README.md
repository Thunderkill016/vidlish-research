# Pronunciation research

Nếp optimizes pronunciation for **intelligibility and comprehensibility**, not accent erasure.

## RQ-007 initial policy

See `SYN-PRN-001` and `FEAT-PRN-001`. Candidate priorities for Vietnamese-speaking beginners are selected by:

```text
Vietnamese population prior
× learner-specific diagnostic
× communicative / functional load
× listener consequence
× practice cost
```

Current strongest candidate domains:

1. information-bearing final consonants/codas and consonant clusters;
2. morphology-bearing final `/s,z/` where direct Vietnamese evidence shows high omission risk;
3. learner-specific high-value consonant/vowel contrasts;
4. lexical stress when stress errors distort vowel/syllable realization or word recognition;
5. sentence focus/timing and interactional prosody when they materially reduce listener effort or change intent.

## Guardrails

- Do not teach the IPA inventory as a fixed curriculum.
- Do not assume all Vietnamese learners share one pronunciation profile.
- Do not correct a reliably intelligible accent solely for native-likeness.
- Do not use a global ASR/vendor score as mastery evidence before RQ-008.
- Keep feature realization, listener intelligibility, comprehensibility and accentedness distinct.

Pronunciation practice should be embedded into `FEAT-SPK-001` and changed-context transfer, rather than isolated from meaningful language use.
