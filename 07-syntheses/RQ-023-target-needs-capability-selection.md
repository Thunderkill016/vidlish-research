# RQ-023 — Target needs and capability selection

**Status:** initial meta-foundation synthesis complete  
**Date:** 2026-08-27  
**Claims:** `CLM-NEED-001`–`CLM-NEED-016`  
**Evidence digest:** `06-evidence/RQ-023-source-digest.md`

## 1. Decision summary

Nếp must not derive the first curriculum from:

```text
CEFR order
word-frequency order
grammar order
textbook topic order
owner intuition
learner skill preference alone
```

The curriculum should ultimately be derived from:

```text
target learner
+
target situations
+
target tasks
+
task importance / frequency / difficulty / training need
+
authentic target discourse / artefacts
+
SLA constraints from RQ-022
→ capability priorities
```

The current published evidence is **not sufficient to freeze the exact first Nếp capability list** for Vietnamese adults near A0.

That is an important research result, not a failure.

## 2. Needs are actions, not school subjects

A weak curriculum asks:

```text
What grammar does A1 study?
What 500 words should Unit 1–20 contain?
What speaking topics are popular?
```

RQ-023 changes the question to:

```text
What does the learner need to accomplish in English?
Under what conditions?
With whom?
Through which channel?
What counts as success?
What language is actually used to do it?
```

Therefore the basic curriculum object should be a **capability grounded in target tasks**, not a topic chapter.

Example:

```text
TOPIC
Restaurants
```

is too weak.

A capability is closer to:

```text
Understand a short order-confirmation question,
provide the missing detail,
and repair one misunderstanding.
```

The task tells us what listening, vocabulary, constructions, pronunciation, interaction and repair are actually needed.

## 3. Published Vietnamese evidence changes the target picture

Across adult-learning and workplace studies, English demand is strongly connected to:

- career and employability;
- practical communication;
- job/study requirements;
- interaction with customers, colleagues or international partners;
- authentic digital and professional texts.

But there is no single “Vietnamese adult English need.”

Different contexts produce materially different profiles.

### Engineering
Needs can combine:
- oral coordination;
- technical reading;
- email/messages;
- instructions/specifications;
- meetings/calls;
- repair across accents and ELF conditions.

### IT / globalized workplaces
Communication includes:
- task coordination;
- relational/pragmatic language;
- written digital communication;
- interaction where context and relationship matter.

### Import/export/business
Needs can include:
- email;
- phone;
- documents/forms;
- requests/confirmations;
- meetings/negotiation;
- functional ELF communication.

### Tourism/hospitality
Oral interaction becomes especially salient:
- requests;
- directions/information;
- reservation/service sequences;
- complaint handling;
- telephone communication;
- intercultural interaction.

Therefore:

```text
one fixed “conversation course”
≠
validated general curriculum
```

## 4. Core + branches is the strongest provisional architecture

Because Nếp targets a broad adult population rather than one profession, RQ-023 supports this provisional structure:

```text
PORTABLE CORE
↓
learner evidence + stated target situations
↓
DOMAIN BRANCHES
```

Possible branch families:

- everyday/public transactions;
- work/employability;
- study/education;
- travel/service;
- digital English;
- occupation-specific English later.

A learner can occupy more than one branch.

This architecture is a research synthesis, not yet a final curriculum graph.

## 5. Candidate portable capabilities to validate

Cross-domain evidence suggests several high-portability candidates.

They should enter the direct needs study as **items to test**, not facts to ship.

### P1 — Exchange essential identifying/context information

Examples:
- name;
- role;
- origin/location;
- contact information;
- basic purpose.

### P2 — Understand and answer a short question/request

The learner must process meaning, not merely match a memorized dialogue.

### P3 — Ask for clarification or repetition

Examples of function:

```text
I didn't catch that.
Could you say that again?
Do you mean ...?
```

Exact language is chosen later by RQ-027.

### P4 — Exchange critical concrete details

Examples:
- numbers;
- prices;
- time/date;
- place;
- quantity;
- contact details.

These details recur in many public and professional tasks and have high error consequences.

### P5 — Make / accept / refuse a simple request

Useful across service, work and personal interaction.

### P6 — Read a short practical text to act

Examples:
- short message;
- instruction;
- label;
- sign;
- simple interface;
- short email fragment.

### P7 — Write a short functional message

Functions:
- request;
- confirm;
- update;
- apologize;
- ask a question.

### P8 — Maintain and close a short interaction

The target is not “small talk” as a topic. It is handling the social structure of a short real interaction.

### P9 — Survive a communication problem

A usable beginner needs a recovery strategy when:
- a word is unknown;
- speech is not understood;
- the interlocutor misunderstands;
- the response differs from the memorized script.

This is important because real language use is not a perfect-dialogue playback system.

## 6. What should NOT become the portable core automatically

Do not promote an item into the core merely because it is common in textbooks.

Examples requiring evidence:

- colors;
- long family-tree vocabulary;
- exhaustive clothing lists;
- zoo animals;
- rare nationalities;
- grammar paradigms taught for completeness;
- isolated pronunciation lists;
- scripted restaurant dialogues with no repair;
- “talk about your hobbies for two minutes” simply because it is easy to make a lesson around.

Some may still be useful language. RQ-027 decides content value after target-task evidence exists.

## 7. Skill balance follows tasks

RQ-023 rejects a universal rule such as:

```text
speaking 50%
listening 30%
reading 10%
writing 10%
```

Skill balance should emerge from target capabilities.

A capability may require:

```text
listen
→ understand intent/detail
→ speak a response
→ interpret repair
```

Another may require:

```text
read message
→ identify requested action
→ write confirmation
```

Therefore Nếp should integrate modalities around target use rather than maintain four disconnected school subjects.

## 8. Accuracy target follows communicative consequence

Vietnam workplace evidence, especially ELF-oriented work, shows that language can succeed without native-like form.

So the product should eventually distinguish:

```text
error that does not block task
vs
error that creates misunderstanding / risk / pragmatic failure
```

This aligns with existing intelligibility and transfer research.

But RQ-023 does not establish a global error-tolerance policy. That belongs partly to RQ-024 and RQ-025.

## 9. Direct target-population research is now a blocker

Before the exact curriculum is frozen, Nếp needs primary data from its intended users.

The direct study must answer:

1. Which English-use situations are likely in the next 6–24 months?
2. What actual tasks occur in those situations?
3. How often?
4. How important are they?
5. What happens if the learner cannot do them?
6. How difficult do novices and experienced users judge them?
7. Which ones require training rather than easy transfer/tool support?
8. Which target tasks recur across domains?
9. Which are domain-specific enough to branch?
10. What authentic language/discourse appears in the highest-priority tasks?

## 10. Dependency on later RQs

RQ-023 selects **what capability matters**.

It does not yet determine:

```text
what observation proves the capability
→ RQ-024

what feedback policy to use
→ RQ-025

what learner differences justify adaptation
→ RQ-026

which words/chunks/constructions instantiate the task
→ RQ-027

how evidence conflicts are represented
→ RQ-028
```

Only after those layers can `SYN-METHOD-001` safely turn target needs into a coherent Nếp Method.

## 11. Current decision

Keep the old RQ-017 curriculum integration as **provisional history**.

Do not build the new Nếp curriculum yet.

RQ-023 contributes this contract:

```text
TARGET TASK
→ CAPABILITY
→ authentic language demands
→ learning design
→ evidence task
```

not:

```text
grammar/topic list
→ lesson
```
