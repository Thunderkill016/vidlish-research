# Product benchmark rules

Product research answers **“how do existing products solve a problem?”**, not automatically **“what causes learning?”**.

## Products worth benchmarking

- Duolingo — course structure, exercise progression, review patterns.
- Language Reactor — replay, caption control, dictionary and source interaction.
- LingQ — learner-owned content and known-word states.
- Readlang — phrase lookup and reading-derived review.
- Anki — review history and scheduling UX.
- LUTE — term state and contextual reading workflow.

## For every benchmark record

Capture:

1. learner problem;
2. exact interaction;
3. likely mechanism;
4. strength;
5. failure mode;
6. evidence status (usually `product benchmark`, not `learning evidence`);
7. what Nếp could test rather than copy.
