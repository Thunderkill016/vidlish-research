# CEFR: what Nếp should and should not use it for

## Claim

CEFR is useful for describing communicative language activities and proficiency with a broader model than four isolated skills. It should not be treated as a teaching algorithm.

## Evidence basis

Primary reference:

- Council of Europe, *Common European Framework of Reference for Languages: Companion Volume*.
- https://rm.coe.int/common-european-framework-of-reference-for-languages-learning-teaching/16809ea0d4

The framework organizes communicative language activity around **reception, production, interaction and mediation** and provides descriptor scales and can-do statements.

## Product implication

Use CEFR to:

- define learner-visible communicative outcomes;
- sanity-check progression coverage;
- maintain a multidimensional capability profile;
- communicate externally when useful.

Do **not** use CEFR alone to:

- decide which vocabulary item comes next;
- determine SRS intervals;
- infer mastery from a single level;
- claim that reaching a feature milestone equals reaching a CEFR level.

## Nếp consequence

Progress should expose evidence of concrete abilities rather than one flattering composite English score. `understood`, `recalled`, `transferred` and `retained` remain separate evidence dimensions.
