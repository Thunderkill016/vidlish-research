# RQ-028 — Source digest: evidence grading, contradiction and update

**Status:** initial meta-foundation evidence digest  
**Date:** 2026-08-27  
**Sources:** `SRC-0343`–`SRC-0356`  
**Claims:** `CLM-META-001`–`CLM-META-022`

## Research question

> How should the knowledge base separately grade methodological quality and directness, represent competing/null findings and boundary conditions, and revise earlier syntheses when stronger/newer evidence appears?

RQ-028 is methodological infrastructure. It does not decide which language-learning intervention wins. It decides what Nếp is allowed to mean when it says evidence is strong.

## 1. Meta-analysis is powerful but not automatically high-certainty

`SRC-0343` describes the major choices and challenges involved in L2 meta-analysis: domain definition, coding, analysis, model choice and interpretation can all change conclusions.

`SRC-0346` empirically reviewed 224 L2 meta-analyses across 99 journals. The review found substantial room for improvement in methodological/reporting practice, including handling of quality, dependence, missing data and generalizability.

`SRC-0347` independently reviewed 120 L2 meta-analyses and similarly identified incomplete reporting, limited quality control and questionable moderator practices.

`SRC-0356` shows why complex primary designs cannot always be represented correctly by simple conventional meta-analytic treatment.

Therefore:

```text
meta-analysis
≠ automatic A-level truth
```

The synthesis itself must be appraised.

## 2. Study quality is multi-dimensional

`SRC-0344` documents weaknesses in quantitative SLA study design, analysis and reporting.

`SRC-0345` proposes a broader view of applied-linguistics study quality including methodological rigor, transparency, ethics and societal value.

`SRC-0352` reinforces that journal metrics/prestige do not substitute for statistical/methodological quality.

For Nếp, methodological quality is therefore kept separate from relevance/directness.

## 3. Directness is a central Nếp problem

`SRC-0351` synthesizes sampling across 308 applied-linguistics research syntheses representing more than 15,000 primary studies and millions of participants. The field remains disproportionately concentrated on relatively young/intermediate university learners, English and a narrow range of institutional/country settings.

This matters because the primary Nếp target is much narrower:

```text
Vietnamese-speaking adults
near zero / Pre-A1
self-study / mobile product
```

A strong study can therefore be:

```text
methodologically strong
+
population-indirect
```

without contradiction.

Construct directness is separate again: a controlled grammar outcome can be direct evidence for that outcome while remaining indirect for spontaneous speaking or real-world transfer.

## 4. GRADE concepts are useful as concepts, not as a medical hierarchy imported whole

`SRC-0353` formalizes certainty domains such as:

- risk of bias;
- inconsistency;
- indirectness;
- imprecision;
- publication bias.

These concepts map well onto problems already encountered in Nếp research.

However, L2 research includes observational, qualitative, psycholinguistic, corpus, assessment and intervention designs. RQ-028 therefore borrows the logic of separate certainty dimensions without mechanically applying a clinical evidence hierarchy.

## 5. Research-synthesis quality also needs transparency

`SRC-0354` (PRISMA 2020) provides a widely adopted systematic-review reporting framework.

`SRC-0348` proposes SMART specifically for applied-linguistics research syntheses and emphasizes systematicity, transparency, accessibility and reflexivity.

`SRC-0355` reviews open-scholarship practices in applied linguistics, including open data/materials, preregistration and replication infrastructure.

These strengthen auditability, but:

```text
transparent process
≠ valid conclusion automatically
```

## 6. Publication bias and null findings cannot be hidden

`SRC-0346` and `SRC-0347` show why publication-bias assessment and completeness of synthesis matter.

RQ-028 therefore requires null, negative and contradictory evidence to remain visible.

A null result may mean:

- little/no effect;
- insufficient precision/power;
- weak manipulation;
- construct mismatch;
- population boundary;
- measurement floor/ceiling.

It must be interpreted, not deleted.

## 7. Replication is a separate confidence signal

`SRC-0349` and `SRC-0350` review replication in second-language research and the methodological role of close/approximate/conceptual replication.

The literature historically contains limited replication, so:

```text
many publications
≠ many independent replications
```

A replication also does not mechanically count as one vote. Similarity of procedures, measures, populations and analysis determines what can be compared.

## 8. Heterogeneity should become boundary-condition knowledge

If a pooled effect is heterogeneous, Nếp should ask:

```text
works for whom?
for which target?
under which task?
with which outcome?
at which delay?
```

`SRC-0343`, `SRC-0346`, `SRC-0347` and `SRC-0356` support careful moderator/sensitivity analysis rather than treating heterogeneity as noise to hide.

This directly motivates `CTR-*` records.

## 9. Required Nếp appraisal axes

RQ-028 adopts five independent axes:

```text
methodological_quality
population_directness
construct_directness
replication_consistency
product_transfer_directness
```

The compact legacy `A–E` claim level remains searchable but is not sufficient for promotion into a high-confidence method rule.

The axes must not be mechanically averaged.

## 10. Claim-level appraisal

Appraisal attaches mainly to claims, not only papers.

The same source can be:

```text
highly direct
for the construct it actually measured
```

and:

```text
highly indirect
for a broader product inference
```

Hence `EVA-*` records point to `CLM-*` plus the relevant source set.

## 11. Contradictions are first-class data

`CTR-*` records preserve:

- competing positions;
- linked claims/sources;
- boundary conditions;
- current bounded resolution;
- evidence that would change the resolution.

Initial examples created in RQ-028:

- explicit instruction advantage vs spontaneous/implicit ability;
- prompts vs recasts/supplied correction;
- lexical coverage threshold vs continuous/modality-specific relationship.

## 12. Evidence changes over time

RQ-028 requires transparent revision rather than silent replacement.

Candidate lifecycle:

```text
ACTIVE
→ NARROWED / REVISED
→ SUPERSEDED
```

Review triggers include:

- stronger meta-analysis/systematic review;
- credible contradictory replication;
- more direct Vietnamese near-A0 evidence;
- direct Nếp-context experiment;
- changed construct-validity interpretation;
- important publication-bias/methodological reassessment.

## 13. Consequence for RQ-001–RQ-027

The earlier cycles remain useful evidence modules.

They are **not invalidated** by RQ-028.

But their legacy `A–E` labels cannot by themselves justify high-confidence `SYN-METHOD-001` statements.

Before a core method rule is promoted, its important supporting claims must be checked for:

```text
quality
+ population directness
+ construct directness
+ consistency
+ product directness
+ unresolved controversy
```

## 14. Main boundary

The protocol is deliberately not a numeric truth machine.

It should answer:

```text
WHAT WE KNOW
WHY WE THINK IT
WHERE IT APPLIES
WHERE IT MAY FAIL
WHAT WOULD CHANGE OUR MIND
```

not:

```text
science score = 87.4
```
