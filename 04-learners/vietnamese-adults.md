# Vietnamese-speaking adults

Nếp initially optimizes for a Vietnamese-speaking adult near A0. This is a product scope that makes L1-aware scaffolding and targeted research valuable.

## What may justify Vietnamese-specific support

Candidate domains include:

- English sound contrasts and final consonants/clusters;
- word stress and rhythm;
- article/tense/construction differences;
- pragmatic/register transfer;
- reliance on Vietnamese translation during early comprehension.

## Example seed source

A study on Vietnamese learners' English word-final /s/ and /z/ can motivate targeted pronunciation research, but it cannot define every Vietnamese learner's profile:

- https://www.mdpi.com/2226-471X/9/10/327

## Guardrail

Never encode “Vietnamese learners cannot X” from one study. Use L1-specific findings to prioritize candidate diagnostics and interventions, then measure the actual learner.

## RQ-002 update — L1 scaffold evidence

Broad L2 evidence now supports **strategic L1 scaffolding** and argues against treating
English-only instruction as a universal requirement, with especially strong evidence for
L1 lexical glosses/explanations in lower-proficiency vocabulary learning (`SYN-SCF-001`).

This does **not** yet establish a Vietnamese-specific causal effect or an exact amount of
Vietnamese support. Nếp should therefore:

- use Vietnamese as a candidate scaffold where it removes irrelevant comprehension cost;
- record the scaffold type/level rather than treating all L1 use as one variable;
- hide answer-bearing Vietnamese when measuring independent English performance;
- calibrate fading on Vietnamese-speaking near-A0 adults through `EXP-002`.

The previous idea of tapering support around a fixed vocabulary count must remain a product
hypothesis until directly validated.
