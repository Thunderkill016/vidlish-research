# EXP-002 — Calibrate Vietnamese scaffold disclosure and fading

## Question

For Vietnamese-speaking near-A0 adults, which scaffold policy gives the best **later
unsupported English performance per minute** without making the first session unusable?

## Why this experiment exists

Research supports strategic L1 use and beginner L1 meaning support, but does not establish:

- the optimal amount of Vietnamese;
- whether translation should be always visible or progressively revealed;
- the exact threshold at which support should fade;
- the best policy for an audio-first web product.

## Candidate conditions

Use comparable learning targets and rotate conditions within/between learners where
carryover can be controlled.

### A — always-on Vietnamese meaning

Target English and Vietnamese meaning/translation are visible immediately.

### B — progressive Vietnamese disclosure

Start with the English task; after an attempt/failure/help request reveal progressively
from context hint → micro-gloss → fuller translation.

### C — minimal Vietnamese

Vietnamese is limited to task instructions; lexical/meaning help uses English, image,
replay or known-context cues before Vietnamese rescue.

## Primary outcome

Delayed **unsupported** English performance on unseen parallel probes.

Measure separately:

- aural meaning comprehension;
- form/meaning recall;
- controlled changed-context use.

## Secondary outcomes

- first-session task completion;
- help requests;
- full-translation reveal rate;
- learning time per target;
- next-day return for probe;
- subjective confusion/load;
- support needed on the delayed probe.

## Important design rule

Do not evaluate the condition using the same support that defined the treatment. The
critical test is later English performance with answer-bearing Vietnamese hidden.

## Fading sub-experiment

For targets successfully learned under condition B, compare candidate suppression rules:

```text
F1: suppress after one unsupported success
F2: suppress after two separated unsupported successes
F3: keep learner-requested support available but stop auto-showing after evidence threshold
```

Exact criteria and sample size must be preregistered before interpreting results.

## Decision rule

Prefer the least intrusive policy that:

1. keeps true beginners able to complete the task;
2. does not materially reduce delayed unsupported performance;
3. minimizes unnecessary support dependence and time cost.

Do not choose a policy because it creates higher immediate accuracy.

## Before running

Pre-register:

- inclusion criteria for "near A0";
- target item sampling;
- minimum sample size/power or Bayesian decision threshold;
- delayed intervals;
- scoring rules;
- stopping/decision criteria;
- privacy/event logging requirements.
