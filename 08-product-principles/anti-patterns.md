# Anti-patterns

Avoid these unless an experiment produces strong contrary evidence.

- **Streak = progress** — engagement is not capability.
- **Completed card = known word** — completion is not recall/retention/transfer.
- **Always-on answer** — undermines tasks intended to measure retrieval.
- **One English score** — hides uneven listening, reading, interaction and support dependence.
- **Video-first A0** — authentic media can exceed lexical and perceptual readiness.
- **Bilingual subtitles always on** — may turn listening into L1 reading.
- **Every unknown word auto-pauses** — plausible UX, but not established learning truth.
- **ASR score = pronunciation ability** — recognition accuracy is not a validated global pronunciation grade.
- **AI explanation = evidence** — model output is not a research source.
- **Competitor has it = we need it** — benchmark is not efficacy evidence.
- **Fixed repetition ritual** — number of repetitions should not replace performance evidence.
- **Native-like accent = goal** — communication/intelligibility is the primary product objective.
